# Pistahan 33 — Station Coordinator Guide

*For the Parade, Festival & Pistahan scanning leads + help desk + parade ops. The app: **pistahan.app/station** (the bare pistahan.app now shows the volunteer guide; your setup link and home-screen icon keep working as before)*

## Set up your phone (do this the night before)

1. Open the **setup link** Grace sends you (it pre-fills the backend
   connection). Use Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Pick your **station** (Parade / Festival / Pistahan / Help desk — or
   **Contingent** / **MC** for parade operations), enter **your name**.
3. Tap **Download roster** — wait for "✓ … volunteers loaded". (Contingent and
   MC stations download the **contingent list** instead.)
4. Tap **Start**.
5. **Add the app to your home screen** (Share → *Add to Home Screen*). This
   keeps it working offline reliably.
6. **Airplane-mode test:** turn airplane mode on, tap Start scanning, scan any
   pass or use Search to check someone in. It must work instantly. Turn
   airplane mode back off — the queued test check-in syncs automatically.
7. Charge the phone to 100% and bring a **power bank**.

## Scanning (the main job)

- Tap **Start scanning** once at the start of your shift (keep the screen on —
  the app holds a wake lock).
- Volunteer shows their QR → hold the phone ~6 inches away, steady.
- **Beep + green card** → call out the **shirt size** (it's huge on screen),
  point them to their **post**. Next!
- **Amber "ALREADY CHECKED IN"** → they're fine, let them through.
- **Red "NOT ON ROSTER"** → send them to the **help desk**. Do not solve it in
  the lane.
- Dark screen or glare? Tap the **🔦 torch** button.

**The 20-second rule: anything that takes longer than 20 seconds goes to the
help desk.** One 3-minute conversation puts your lane 10 people behind.

## When the QR won't scan

- **Type the code** printed under their QR (like `PST-4K7Q`) into the box
  under the viewfinder → Go.
- **No pass at all:** **Search** tab → type their last name → tap **Check in**.
  ~10 seconds.

## Help desk only

- **Walk-up** tab: add people who aren't on the roster (name, phone, shirt,
  post). They sync to the Walkups list automatically.

## Contingent station (parade ops coordinators only)

For the coordinator crew tracking parade contingents at staging.

- **Contingents tab:** search by **cluster code** (like `A5`), name, or
  contingent number. The list is in parade order.
- Tap a contingent to open its full details: **primary contact (tap the phone
  number to call)**, participants, vehicles, staging instructions, and notes.
- When a contingent checks into staging, tap **Mark arrived** — it logs the
  time and syncs to the Google Sheet (works offline, just like scans).
- **Register tab:** a contingent that isn't on the list? Add it here (name,
  contact, cluster, vehicles). It syncs for the parade team to place.

## MC station (announcer's iPad or iPhone)

Read-only and built for the main stage — big type, works fully offline.

- As a contingent approaches, type its **cluster code** (like `A5`) — the card
  shows the contingent's name, number, **bio, and fun facts** to read out.
- **Prev / Next** walk the parade order, and "Up next" shows who's coming, so
  you never lose your place. Tapping any row in the list jumps to it.
- Set it up the night before like any station (pick **MC**, download the
  contingent list). If the parade order changes morning-of, tap ⚙ → reset →
  re-download while you still have good internet.

## The status bar (top of screen)

- **`126 / 194`** — checked in so far (this device's count) with progress bar.
- **`online · synced`** — all check-ins are in the Google Sheet.
- **`offline · 4 queued`** — totally fine! Check-ins are saved on the phone
  and upload themselves when signal returns. **Never stop scanning because
  you're offline.** Tap the pill to force a sync.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Camera won't start | Use manual code entry or the Search tab |
| App won't load | Use the spare phone (already set up); ferry people there |
| "Backend down!" | Irrelevant during the rush — everything queues locally. Do **not** stop to fix it. |
| Wrong station/name on this phone | ⚙ gear → reset → set up again (roster and queued check-ins are kept) |
| Everything is on fire | Paper roster + highlighter, reconcile later |

## Never do

- Don't hand your phone to volunteers.
- Don't troubleshoot in the lane (help desk exists for that).
- Don't clear the browser's website data — that deletes queued check-ins.
